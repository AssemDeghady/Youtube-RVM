## Detailed Description

**Effortlessly bulk-link your YouTube Shorts to long-form videos directly from YouTube Studio!**

YouTube Shorts are one of the most powerful discovery engines on YouTube, but manually linking a "Related Video" to each Short one by one is tedious, repetitive, and time-consuming.

**ShortLinker** solves this problem by allowing creators to bulk-assign a single Related Video across dozens of YouTube Shorts at once—directly from the YouTube Studio Shorts listing page, without opening individual video editors!

---

### Key Features

- ⚡ **Listing-Page Bulk Assignment:** Update 10, 30, or more Shorts in seconds while staying on the YouTube Studio Shorts listing page (`Content → Shorts`).
- 🎯 **Native Studio Checkbox Integration:** Select the Shorts you want to update using Studio's built-in checkboxes. ShortLinker automatically detects your selection.
- 🔍 **Interactive Target Video Picker:** Choose your target video directly from your channel's recent video catalog or paste any public YouTube video link/ID.
- ⏱️ **Safe Sequential Execution:** Updates are queued and processed sequentially with automated delays to respect YouTube Studio service limits.
- 🛑 **Live Progress & Stop Control:** Track progress in real time with an itemized progress bar. Pause or stop the queue at any time with a single click.
- 🔄 **One-Click Retry:** Encountered a temporary network glitch on a specific Short? Click "Retry Failed" to re-process only the affected items.
- 🔒 **Zero Credential Persistence & 100% Private:** Operates strictly within your active, authenticated YouTube Studio browser tab. No passwords, API keys, or cookies are collected, stored, or sent to any external server.

---

### How to Use ShortLinker

1. Open **[YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com)** and navigate to **Content → Shorts**.
2. **One-Time Calibration:** Open any Short in Studio, select a Related Video, and click **Save**. ShortLinker detects this action to capture your session's update template in temporary browser memory.
3. Return to the **Shorts** list view and select the Shorts you want to link using Studio's checkboxes.
4. In the ShortLinker panel (bottom right), pick your target video from your catalog or enter a video URL.
5. Click **Apply to Selected Shorts**.
6. Sit back and watch ShortLinker sequentially update each Short while you remain on the listing page!

ShortLinker is built with a strict **Zero-Data-Collection** philosophy:
